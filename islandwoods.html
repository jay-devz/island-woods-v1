Division 1

Scaffolding Rental.

Weekly and monthly scaffold rental for builders, contractors, and homeowners. Delivery, assembly, and inspection by IslandWoods.

How That Works

Four steps, no guesswork.

Tell us the job: height, footprint, duration, and we handle transport, assembly, and safety sign-off.

01Call or submit a request with your site dimensions and rental dates.
02We confirm a scaffold plan and schedule delivery to your site.
03Our riggers erect and safety-tag the structure before you start work.
04When the job's done, we strike it down and collect on schedule.

Division 2

Roofing Membranes.

Waterproof torch-on and liquid-applied membrane systems for flat and low-slope roofs, built to handle heavy rain.

How That Works

Inspect, prep, seal, warranty.

Every roofing job starts with an on-site inspection so we can recommend the right membrane for your roof deck and drainage.

01We inspect the existing roof deck, drainage, and any leak history.
02We recommend a membrane type and share a written estimate.
03Our crew strips, preps, and seals the roof deck edge to edge.
04You get a signed workmanship warranty and care guide.

About Us

Jamaican-owned.
Built on integrity.

Founded by Project Engineer Michael Ranger, Island Woods & Development Ltd. is a trusted, award-winning Jamaican general contractor with over two decades of engineering excellence. Registered with the National Contracts Commission, we deliver premier commercial construction, heavy equipment and scaffolding rentals, and bulk roofing membrane supply across the region.

Driven by modern technology and a skilled technical leadership team, Island Woods combines proven project management with high-caliber execution to build the infrastructure that moves the Caribbean forward.
